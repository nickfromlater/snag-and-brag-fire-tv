## Adaptations and limits

The audio functions and initializer bytes are unchanged. The adapter temporarily uses an
offline constructor/proxy and disables the wall-clock scheduler, restores seeded RNG
after rendering, and forces the declared 102 bpm lobby rather than its initial 104 bpm
placeholder. Cut the middle of three loops, rounding fractional boundaries to samples.
No seam repair, added fade, individual normalization or changed sound parameter was needed.
The lead-authorized makeup gain is the only export-level loudness change and is applied
after the original trim/cut, before quantization, with one factor for the entire bank.

An additional offline adapter is necessary for byte identity: junctions of more than two
source connections use balanced unity-gain binary sums. Seeded unadapted composites varied
by one PCM16 count; Chromium's pointer-hash connection order is consistent with that
observation. The adapter preserves every original synthesis/filter/envelope node and the
master/reverb chain, adding no delay or signal shaping. The repeated probes and full
regeneration check the fix; primary source links are in the renderer README.
Before makeup, the complete comparison with unadapted source PCM found no difference larger than one
16-bit count, and all attacks, durations and bar offsets are unchanged.

The worker brief's finite variants quantize otherwise continuous mtick/crowd input.
Selecting mash files by tap count `%13` preserves source pitch, but at tap counts above 12
the original noise filter would keep rising; the finite bank restarts it. The three
tomato WAVs are identical because their source has no randomness. No extra files or
redesigned sounds were added to disguise those facts.

Mix reference: SFX .8, music .22, master .9, reverb .18 wet; master compressor threshold
-12 dB, ratio 4, Web Audio default knee 30 dB / attack .003 s / release .25 s. These gains
and per-file compression remain baked in, followed by the uniform makeup gain. The TV
plays every file at unity (1.0), summed then passed to its soft limiter. Sound/Music
on-off toggles and optional user volume are the only gain controls. Original bus levels
and makeup must not be applied again at playback. An isolated mastered file cannot reconstruct the original
compressor reacting to simultaneous sounds. Fanfare already embeds crowd(2,.18), and
snagged already embeds shutter. Avoid layering those embedded effects twice.

The ebur128 floor of -70 LUFS for effects under 400 ms is recorded and marked
`insufficient-duration`, rather than treating it as measured loudness or changing the
audio to satisfy the meter. Reproducibility is tied to the source, seed and Chrome/ffmpeg
versions recorded in the manifest.
