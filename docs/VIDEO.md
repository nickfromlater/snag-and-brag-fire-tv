# The video: storyboard v1 (Oct 12)

The video is the entry. Judges may stop at 3:00 and may judge on the video alone, so it
must prove three things in the first 25 seconds: it runs on a Fire TV, phones are the
controllers, and it is funny. Target length is **2:50**.

**Capture plan, assuming no HDMI capture card:**
- **Room camera** (a phone on a tripod, 4K30, locked off, framing the TV and the couch).
  Every section opens on a room shot of the real stick on the real TV. That shot is the
  stage 1 proof.
- **Clean TV footage** from the device's own screen recording if the spike finds one.
  Otherwise from the Vega simulator (the rules allow it), recorded at 1080p and cut as
  inserts that match the room shot.
- **Phone footage** from iOS screen recording on two players' phones, plus over-the-shoulder
  camera shots.
- **Sound:** game audio from the clean capture, mixed under room audio (the laughter). No
  outside music. Everything we use is synthesized by the game.
- **Captions** burned in, short, in the game's type (Lilita One on a paper pill with an
  ink border).
- **Set prep:** no brand logos in shot. Tape the TV's maker logo, hide branded boxes and
  bottles, and stock a "hero objects" basket (a red mug, a rubber duck, a teddy, an old
  portrait, a frying pan, a weird sock). Everyone on camera agrees to appear.
